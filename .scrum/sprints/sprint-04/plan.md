### T04-04 — Terminal task and calendar visibility
**Agent**: dev-2 | **BLI**: BLI-029 | **Estimate**: M

- [ ] `/today` chat command:
  - Shows today's Google Calendar events (loaded from `datainput/googlecalendar.yml`)
  - Shows tasks due today from Obsidian + LogSeq + Apple Reminders (tasks with `📅 YYYY-MM-DD` matching today)
  - Overdue tasks (past due date) highlighted in red
  - Rendered as a Rich table: Time | Event/Task | Source
- [ ] `/week` chat command:
  - 7-day Rich table: Date | Day | Calendar Events | Tasks Due
  - Counts shown, not full text (keeps it compact)
- [ ] `python main.py --today` runs `/today` output non-interactively and exits
- [ ] `scripts/remind.py "Task text" "HH:MM"`:
  - Schedules a macOS notification at HH:MM using `at` command + `osascript`
  - Prints: `Reminder set: "Task text" at HH:MM`
  - Falls back to `print` warning if not on macOS or `at` not available
- [ ] `scripts/remind.py` documented in `INSTALL.md` under new section "Terminal Reminders"
